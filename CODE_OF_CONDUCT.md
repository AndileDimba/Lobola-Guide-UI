# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## This project in particular

Lobola Hub is about people's families, heritage and language. That raises the
bar on how we talk to each other:

- **Discuss it the way you would in someone's home.** Lobola is a living
  practice, not a curiosity to be dissected. Contributions and reviews that
  treat it as backward, quaint or transactional are not welcome here.
- **Disagreement about practice is normal.** Families and regions do things
  differently. "That is not how my family does it" is useful information, not an
  accusation — and it is answered by softening the framing, not by arguing about
  who is right.
- **Correcting someone's isiZulu is a kindness, not a put-down** — offer it as
  one, and receive it as one.
- **Respect what is not yours to share.** Do not publish another family's or
  community's private practice, and do not pressure a contributor to disclose
  more than they are comfortable putting on a public site.

## Expected behaviour

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the people who will read this site, not just for
  us as individuals

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Mockery or denigration of a culture, language, family practice or belief
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit
or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests and discussions — and also when someone is officially representing
the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
maintainer, [@AndileDimba](https://github.com/AndileDimba). Serious incidents
can also be reported directly to GitHub via
[Report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporters'
privacy and security will be respected.
