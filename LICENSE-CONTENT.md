## What counts as content

Anything that carries the teaching rather than the machinery:

- The stage narratives, descriptions and beats (English and isiZulu) in `src/data/`
- Izithakazelo, spoken phrases and their English glosses
- Role names and glosses, glossary entries
- FAQ questions and answers
- Illustrations, photography and captions authored for this project
- The written documents in `docs/`

Everything else — React components, hooks, Tailwind config, build and deploy
setup — is code, under MIT.

## What CC BY-SA 4.0 means in practice

You are free to **share** (copy and redistribute) and **adapt** (remix,
translate, build upon) this content, for any purpose including commercial, so long as you:

- **Attribute** — credit Lobola Hub and link back to the source and the license.
- **ShareAlike** — if you build on this content, release your version under
  CC BY-SA 4.0 as well. The content stays open for the next person.
- **No additional restrictions** — you may not add DRM or legal terms that stop
  others doing what this license permits.

This is deliberate. The content should be free to translate, teach from and
carry into other communities — and it should stay that way rather than being
enclosed in someone's closed product.
